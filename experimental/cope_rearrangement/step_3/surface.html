Reading it

Hover or tap a surface to show the nearest grid point. A stationary-point marker shows step 4b's optimised geometry where 4b located that point, and the nearest grid point's where it did not. Click to pin, and click again to unpin. With a surface focused, ←→↑↓ move the point and Enter pins it. The diagonal cut works the same way: hover a point on either curve. Drag the molecule to rotate it. The faint crosshair in the other panel marks the same (R16, R34).

What the shapes say

PC-NEVPT2 has one stationary point, a saddle on the diagonal at 1.922 Å: the concerted TS. CASSCF has a diyl minimum at 1.649 Å, two off-diagonal saddles beside it, a maximum at 1.888 Å and the aromatic saddle at 2.269 Å, so its route is stepwise.

The mirrored half

Only R16 ≤ R34 was run. The chair's inversion swaps the two bonds, so a point above the diagonal shows its mirror's geometry with C1↔C3 and C4↔C6 relabelled. Geometries are rotated onto the TS's carbon frame so neighbouring points line up.
