# Integrals

Gaussian integrals over a `BasisSet`, on the GPU, with kernels generated by
[FelizJueves](../../deps/FelizJueves) (a submodule at `deps/FelizJueves`).

## Module

`free_breakfast.integrals`, which re-exports `mmd`, `one_electron`,
`eri.integral_direct.coulomb` (and through it
`eri.integral_direct.coulomb.pair_class`), `eri.integral_direct.exchange`
(and through it `eri.integral_direct.exchange.pairs`),
`eri.integral_direct.nonsymmetric_jk`, `eri.density_fitting` and
`active_space`.

```
active_space/  free_breakfast.integrals.active_space: the active-space Hamiltonian (E_core, h_eff, (tu|vw)) of a set of orbitals, from one J and K of the core and one J per active pair; and casscf_integrals, a CASSCF's (pq|uv) and (pu|qv) over every orbital, from the same J and one K per part of each active pair
mmd/           free_breakfast.integrals.mmd: McMurchie-Davidson primitive-pair records, built on the host, ordered by zeta on the device
one_electron/  free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron: the one-electron matrices S, T, V and H = T + V on the GPU
  launch/            free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.launch: the per-class launch and the pre-launch checks
  overlap/           free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.overlap: the overlap S
  overlap_mixed/     free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.overlap_mixed: S between two bases
  kinetic/           free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.kinetic: the kinetic energy T
  potential_en/      free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.potential_en: the electron-nuclear potential V
  core_hamiltonian/  free_breakfast.integrals.one_electron.core_hamiltonian: H = T + V
eri/           the electron-repulsion integrals
  density_fitting/   free_breakfast.integrals.eri.density_fitting: the aggregate over metric, three_center, fit, jk and mo (the Coulomb metric (P|Q), the three-center (mn|P), the fit B = L^-1 (mn|P), DF J and K, B in the MO basis over column ranges of C)
    metric/          free_breakfast::integrals::eri::density_fitting::metric::device: the generated Coulomb metric kernels metric<lP, lQ>, one thread per aux shell pair, 28 classes lP <= lQ through i
    three_center/    free_breakfast.integrals.eri.density_fitting.three_center: the spherical three-center (mn|P) on the GPU over the generated eri3<la, lb, lP>, one thread per (AO shell pair, aux shell), 70 classes la <= lb through f, lP through i
  integral_direct/   J and K contracted from the integrals as they are formed, never stored
    coulomb/         free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.coulomb: the Coulomb matrix J of an AO density on the GPU, Schwarz-screened, over its own device-only classes: mmd's records with the negligible primitive pairs pruned on the device
      pair_class/    free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.coulomb.pair_class: J's device-only classes, CoulombPairClass, and the prune that makes them from mmd's
      dq/            free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.coulomb.dq: the ket pre-contraction D_q and Dmax, over the generated dq<lc, ld>
      e_hermite/     free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.coulomb.e_hermite: the screened [p|q] contraction into each bra's J_p, over the generated e_hermite<lp, lq>
      j_coulomb/     free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.coulomb.j_coulomb: the bra back-transform and scatter of J_p into J, over the generated jab<la, lb>
      sort_bounds/   free_breakfast::integrals::eri::integral_direct::coulomb::sort_bounds::device: each ket class sorted by its screen bound Iq * Dmax, into the class's own ket_ fields
    exchange/        free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.exchange: the exchange matrix K of an AO density on the GPU, screened by the Schwarz bound and the density's decay
      helpers/       free_breakfast::integrals::eri::integral_direct::exchange::helpers: max|D|, the Dcd gather, the block table and its density factors, the decay they are cut from, the shell starts and the mirror kb leaves to its launcher
      kernels/kb/    free_breakfast::integrals::eri::integral_direct::exchange::device: the generated K kernels kb<la, lc, lb, ld>, one warp per K_ab block, 81 classes through d
      pairs/         free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.exchange.pairs: mmd's records in both orientations, one device-only class per ordered (la, lb), grouped by first shell, each with K's dressed record bound (mmd's bound_ab or bound_ba), each group sorted by it
    nonsymmetric_jk/ free_breakfast.integrals.eri.integral_direct.nonsymmetric_jk: G = J[D] - K[D]/2 and K[D] of a non-symmetric density (a CIS transition density), from one coulomb of its symmetric part and an exchange sweep of each part
shared/        free_breakfast::integrals::shared::headers: device headers more than one kernel family includes (eval_boys.h)
```

## How the pieces fit

```
BasisSet --primitive_pair_classes--> PrimitivePairClass[]          (mmd, on the device, once per basis)
         --overlap / kinetic-->      CartesianMatrixAO S, T        (one launch per class, each on its own stream)
Molecule(res, atoms)                  nuclei on the device          (molecule, once per geometry)
         --nuclear_attraction-->     CartesianMatrixAO V           (one launch per class)
         --core_hamiltonian-->       CartesianMatrixAO H = T + V   (two launches per class, one matrix)
PrimitivePairClass[] --coulomb_pair_classes--> CoulombPairClass[]  (coulomb, a device copy without the pairs J cannot need, assuming |D| <= 2, once per basis)
CartesianMatrixAO D                   an AO density
         --coulomb-->                CartesianMatrixAO J           (ket-major: dq, the ket sort and e_hermite against every bra per class, then jab per class)
PrimitivePairClass[] --ordered_pair_classes--> OrderedPairClass[]  (exchange.pairs, both orientations, grouped by first shell, each group sorted by mmd's dressed bound and gathered on the device, once per basis)
CartesianMatrixAO D --exchange-->    CartesianMatrixAO K           (the decay and Dcd per class, then kb per (bra class, ket class), one warp per a <= b block, and the mirror)
non-symmetric D --nonsymmetric_jk--> G = J - K/2, K              (D's symmetric and antisymmetric parts by geam; exchange of each, coulomb of the symmetric one)
orbitals C, ncore, ncas --active_space_hamiltonian--> E_core, h_eff, (tu|vw)  (J and K of the core density, then one J per active pair v <= w)
         --casscf_integrals-->       the same, and (pq|uv), (pu|qv) over all p, q  (the same J onto all of C, then one K per pair's symmetric part and one per u < v of its antisymmetric part)
         --MatrixAOPair::cartesian_to_spherical-->  spherical      (recipes.matrix_ao)
```

**Generated kernels are committed, not built.** `tools/emit/one_electron_kernels.py`,
`tools/emit/coulomb_kernels.py`, `tools/emit/exchange_kernels.py` and
`tools/emit/df_kernels.py` write them
from the FelizJueves submodule; a build never runs Python. Re-run all four after
bumping the submodule. Each family has its own directory
(`one_electron/{overlap,kinetic,potential_en}/kernels/`,
`mmd/kernels/pair_bound/`,
`eri/integral_direct/coulomb/{dq,e_hermite,j_coulomb}/kernels/`,
`eri/integral_direct/exchange/kernels/{kb,kbr}/`, `mmd/kernels/record_bound/`,
`eri/density_fitting/{metric,three_center}/kernels/`), and
the device Boys routine every unit with a fundamental includes, `eval_boys<M>`,
is generated once into `shared/eval_boys.h` rather than carried in each unit,
beside the
`shared/eval_boys_{M}.h` it includes: below its switch, one fixed `fma` chain
per order M <= 20 in place of a summed loop. Each of those
directories has a `.clang-format` that turns formatting off, and
`devtools/check-int-types.py` skips a file by FelizJueves's first-line banner:
the files stay byte-for-byte as emitted, so a fix belongs in the generator.

**One class per unordered pair of momenta.** `mmd` swaps every shell pair into
`la <= lb`, so `(s|p)` has a kernel and `(p|s)` does not need one. K is the
exception: it is not symmetric inside a pair, so `exchange/pairs` holds both
orientations, and its record bound has a kernel per ordered class. The
matrices are symmetric and the kernels write each value at `(m, n)` and
`(n, m)`, so the swap changes nothing about the result.

**Not done, in J and K alike: batching.** Each build's scratch is its
classes', allocated once per basis, and each launches one kernel per class (or
class pair, or ket group) as FelizJueves ships it. K applies the bra-ket swap
by its shell blocks, computing `a <= b` and mirroring (negated for an
antisymmetric density), so it still visits the quartets inside its `a == b`
blocks twice
([`exchange/`](eri/integral_direct/exchange/README.md)).

## Tests

`test/gtest/mmd_gpu.cpp` (the records), `test/gtest/overlap_gpu.cpp` (S) and
`test/gtest/core_hamiltonian_gpu.cpp` (T, V and H), against libcint and PySCF's
`hcore` through the golden data in `test/gtest/data/one_electron/`, and
`test/gtest/coulomb_gpu.cpp` (J), against libcint's `vj` through
`test/gtest/data/coulomb/`, `test/gtest/exchange_gpu.cpp` (K), against
libcint's `vk` through `test/gtest/data/exchange/`,
`test/gtest/nonsymmetric_jk_gpu.cpp` (J - K/2 and K of a non-symmetric
density), against libcint's `vj` and `vk` of a random one through
`test/gtest/data/exchange/*_nonsymmetric.json`, and
`test/gtest/active_space_gpu.cpp` (the active-space Hamiltonian, and
CASSCF's ppaa and papa), against PySCF's CASCI and `CASSCF.ao2mo` through `test/gtest/data/active_space/`.
